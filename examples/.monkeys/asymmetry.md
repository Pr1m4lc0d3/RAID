# Asymmetry — ground they cannot hold

## Incumbents

- Scovie Club (fictional hot-sauce-of-the-month box) — revenue model: monthly subscription box, billed automatically until cancelled — therefore cannot say: "no commitment, cancel anytime" as a lead claim, and cannot say "buy exactly one bottle" without cannibalizing the box that pays their bills

## Our ground

- "one bottle, no subscription, no auto-renewal, ever" — because: Backlot has no recurring-revenue line to protect, so the claim costs it nothing a subscription incumbent could match without changing their business model
